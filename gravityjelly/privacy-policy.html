GravityJelly Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 20, 2026

This policy explains how the Android game GravityJelly ("the game"), made by PxlZoid ("we"), handles information. In short: we do not collect or store any personal information. The game shows ads from Google AdMob, and Google receives limited technical information to show those ads, as described below.

Information we collect

None. The game has no accounts, no sign-in, no analytics and no in-app purchases. It does not ask for your name, email, location, contacts, photos or any other personal information, and we have no servers that receive data from the game.

Ads

The game shows ads from Google AdMob: an optional video you can choose to watch for a reward (an undo, extra moves or a bomb), and a full-screen ad between levels after every few cleared levels. To request and show ads, the AdMob SDK sends Google technical information such as the device model, Android version, IP address (used to estimate the country), app version and diagnostic and ad interaction data (for example, whether an ad was shown or tapped).

Every ad request from the game is marked as directed to children and limited to ads rated for general audiences (G). This means Google does not show personalized (interest-based) ads or remarketing in the game, and the game does not use the Android advertising ID. Google's use of this information is described in Google's Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Information stored on your device

Your game progress (cleared levels, stars, pets, drops, shop items) and your settings (sound, vibration, tilt) are saved in a file on your device. We never receive it and cannot see it. If you have Android backup turned on, Android may include this file in your device backup to your own Google account, so your progress comes back when you reinstall or move to a new phone; that backup is handled by Google and we cannot access it. You can delete the file by uninstalling the game or clearing the game's storage in Android settings.

Device features

  • Motion sensor: if you switch on "Tilt to play", the game reads the phone's accelerometer to detect a tilt. The readings are used on the device at that moment and are never stored or sent anywhere.
  • Vibration: the game uses the vibration permission for short haptic pulses, which you can switch off in Settings.

Children

The game is suitable for players of all ages, including children. Because it collects no personal information from anyone, it collects none from children. Ads are child-directed as described in "Ads" above: no personalized ads, no advertising ID, and only ads rated for general audiences. The game contains no links to social media.

Google Play

The game is distributed through Google Play. Google may collect information when you download or use apps from Google Play (for example, install statistics and crash reports), as described in Google's Privacy Policy. Google shows us install statistics and crash reports (device model, Android version and the technical error), which do not identify you.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of the game changes how information is handled, we will update this page before that version is released and change the effective date above.
