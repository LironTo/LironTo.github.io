GravityJelly Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 4, 2026

This policy explains how the Android game GravityJelly ("the game"), made by PxlZoid ("we"), handles information. In short: the game does not collect, store on our servers, or share any personal information.

Information we collect

None. The game has no accounts, no sign-in, no ads, no analytics and no in-app purchases. It does not ask for your name, email, location, contacts, photos or any other personal information, and it does not send any data over the internet.

Information stored on your device

Your game progress (cleared levels, stars, pets, drops, shop items) and your settings (sound, vibration, tilt) are saved in a file on your device. We never receive it and cannot see it. If you have Android backup turned on, Android may include this file in your device backup to your own Google account, so your progress comes back when you reinstall or move to a new phone; that backup is handled by Google and we cannot access it. You can delete the file by uninstalling the game or clearing the game's storage in Android settings.

Device features

  • Motion sensor: if you switch on "Tilt to play", the game reads the phone's accelerometer to detect a tilt. The readings are used on the device at that moment and are never stored or sent anywhere.
  • Vibration: the game uses the vibration permission for short haptic pulses, which you can switch off in Settings.

Children

The game is suitable for players of all ages, including children. Because it collects no personal information from anyone, it collects none from children. It shows no ads and contains no links to social media.

Google Play

The game is distributed through Google Play. Google may collect information when you download or use apps from Google Play (for example, install statistics and crash reports), as described in Google's Privacy Policy. Google shows us install statistics and crash reports (device model, Android version and the technical error), which do not identify you.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of the game changes how information is handled (for example, by adding ads), we will update this page before that version is released and change the effective date above.
